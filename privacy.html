隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026-11-24

中文

  • 本應用程式（rclone-xqdata）僅供擁有者個人使用，不對外提供服務。
  • 只要求 Google Drive 唯讀權限（drive.readonly），用來把擁有者自己的雲端資料夾下載到擁有者自己的電腦。
  • 不會修改、刪除或上傳任何雲端檔案；不會收集、儲存、分享或出售任何使用者資料給第三方。
  • 授權憑證只儲存在擁有者的電腦上（rclone 設定檔）。可隨時到 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」撤銷授權。

English

  • This application (rclone-xqdata) is for the owner's personal use only and is not offered to anyone else.
  • It requests read-only Google Drive access (drive.readonly) solely to download the owner's own Drive folder to the owner's own computer.
  • It does not modify, delete or upload any files, and does not collect, store, share or sell any user data to third parties.
  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer (rclone config). Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account third-party access settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
